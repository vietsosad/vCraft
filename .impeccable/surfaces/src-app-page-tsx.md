## Direction contract
THESIS: vCraft is the ceramic mosaic road along the Red River: every workshop, piece and batch is a glazed tile set into one shared grout grid by the village. Refuses the cream-plus-serif artisan marketplace with a terracotta accent.
OWN-WORLD: ngà ground crossed by a visible warm-grey grout grid; whole regions glazed as tiles of Lam Bát Tràng, men ngọc, đất nung and vàng rơm with crackle and hoa văn lam pattern tiles; 4-8px tile corners; vermilion seal for verification. Phudu caps as tile-set signage, Alegreya for names and voice, Be Vietnam Pro for work, JetBrains Mono for codes. Raises: hover glazes a tile with a specular sweep (from glazier partition); status carried by mark shape as well as hue (from jackfield); kiln gauge needle with real damping ballistics on trace (from VU bridge).
STORY: visitor sees the village assembled from real pieces, understands each tile is verifiable, then opens the exhibition, the map, or a wholesale request; each role then works in its own zone.
FIRST VIEWPORT: full-width mosaic wall of product photos and glaze tiles assembling into the grout grid; a lam signage tile holds the Phudu headline and slogan, an open-workshops tile pulses live, a QR tile invites a trace; primary action "Vào phòng triển lãm" on the headline tile, secondary "Đặt hàng sỉ".
